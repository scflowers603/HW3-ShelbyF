1. Briefly describe one important lesson about modularity that the paper describes and that
you think is still relevant today.
Its almost always incorrect to to begin the decomposition of a system into modules on the bassi
of a flowchart. This is because flowcharts are intrinsically procedural and tie module boundaries
to the temporary steps of execution rather than the underlying structure of the data and design.
